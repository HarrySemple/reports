Corollary 1.5.10. Rates of Convergence.

Let \(k\geq 1\) denote the piecewise polynomial degree of elements of \(V_h\text{.}\) Suppose the exact solution \(u\in H_0^r(\Omega)\) of(1.2.1) is such that \(r\) is sufficiently large for \(u\) to belong to the domain of the Lagrange interpolation operator. Then, there exists a constant \(C\) such that
\begin{equation} \enorm{u-u_h} \leq C h^{\min\{k+1,r\} - 1/2} |u|_{H^r(\Omega)}.\tag{1.5.100} \end{equation}
