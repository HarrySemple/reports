Corollary 1.5.6. Coercivity of \(a-s\).

Suppose
\begin{equation} \label{eqn:a-s-coer-1} C_s \leq \frac{1}{2}.\tag{1.5.78} \end{equation}
Then the bilinear form \(a-s\) is coercive. That is, there exists an \(\alpha>0\) such that for all \(v\in V_h\)
\begin{equation} a(v,v) - s(v,v) \geq \alpha \enorm{v}^2.\tag{1.5.79} \end{equation}
