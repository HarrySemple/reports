Corollary 1.6.3. Error Estimate for the Steady State.

Suppose \(u\in H^1_0(\Omega)\) is the solution of (1.2.1) and \(u_h\) the solution of (1.6.5). Given that \(\mathcal{K}\in L^\infty(\Omega\times\Omega)\) and \(C_s\leq 1\text{,}\) we have with probability at least \(1-\eta\)
\begin{equation} \enorm{u-u_h} \leq \frac{1+C_a}{1-C_s} C h^{\min\{k+1,r\} - 1/2} |u|_{H^r(\Omega)} + \frac{B}{1-C_s} \sqrt{\frac{2}{M}\log\left(\frac{2}{\eta}\right)}\tag{1.6.34} \end{equation}
