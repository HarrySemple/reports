Theorem 1.6.1. Strang-Type Error Estimate.

Let \(u\in H^1_0(\Omega)\) be the solution of (1.2.1) and \(u_h\) the solution of (1.6.5). Then,
\begin{equation} \enorm{u - u_h} \leq (1 + C_a)\, \inf_{w_h \in V_h} \snorm{u - w_h} + \sup_{v_h \in V_h} \frac{\left| s(u, v_h) - s_M(u_h, v_h) \right|}{\enorm{v_h}}.\tag{1.6.6} \end{equation}
