Theorem 1.5.9. C\’{e}a-type best-approximation estimate.

Let \(u\in H^1_0\) be a solution of(1.2.1) with \(g=0\) and let \(u_h\in V_h\) be a solution of(1.4.8). Then we have the following best approximation result:
\begin{equation} \enorm{u - u_h} \leq \left(1 + \frac{C_A}{\alpha}\right) \inf_{w_h \in V_h} \snorm{u - w_h}.\tag{1.5.88} \end{equation}
