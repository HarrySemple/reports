Lemma 1.5.11. Contraction in mixed norms.

Let \(u_h\in V_h\) be the solution of the stationary finite element problem(1.4.8). Take \(u_h^{(0)}\in V_h\) and denote by \(\{u_h^{(n)}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}_0}\) the sequence of solutions to the iterative problem(1.4.9). We have that, for all \(n\in \mathbb{N}_0\)
\begin{equation} \enorm{u_h-u_h^{(n)}} \leq 2\tilde{C}_s \ltwo{u_h - u_h^{(n-1)}}.\tag{1.5.109} \end{equation}
