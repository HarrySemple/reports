Corollary 1.6.4. Convergence in SUPG-Norm with MC Approximation.

Let \(u_h\) be the solution of (1.6.5), \(u_h^{(0)}\in V_h\) arbitrary and \(\{u_h^{(n)}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\) the sequence defined by (1.6.2). Given \(\mathcal{K}\in L^\infty(\Omega\times\Omega)\text{,}\) we have for all \(n\in\mathbb{N}_0\text{,}\)
\begin{equation} \enorm{u_h - u_h^{(n)}} \leq (2C_s^M)^n \enorm{u_h - u_h^{(0)}}.\tag{1.6.38} \end{equation}
