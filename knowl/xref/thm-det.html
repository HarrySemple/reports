Theorem 1.5.14. Error Estimate in the Deterministic Setting.

Let \(u\) be the solution of(1.2.1) and suppose \(u_h\in V_h\) is the solution of the stationary finite element problem(1.4.8). Take \(u_h^{(0)}\in V_h\) and denote by \(\{u_h^{(n)}\}_{n\in\mathbb{N}_0}\) the sequence of solutions to the iterative problem(1.4.9). We have that, for all \(n\in \mathbb{N}_0\)
\begin{equation} \enorm{u-u_h^{(n)}} \leq C h^{\min\{k+1,r\} - 1/2} |u|_{H^r(\Omega)} + 2\tilde{C}_s \left( \frac{2\tilde{C}_s}{\mu^{1/2}} \right)^{n-1} \ltwo{u_h - u_h^{(0)}}.\tag{1.5.121} \end{equation}
